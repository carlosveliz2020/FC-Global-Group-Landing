About Us

Built on Discipline.
Driven by Data.

FC Global Group LLC was founded by Carlos and Fabiola Veliz with a clear mission: to build a wholesale distribution operation that suppliers can trust. Based in Knoxville, Tennessee, we specialize in sourcing authorized products and distributing them through Amazon's FBA infrastructure.

Our approach is methodical. Every product we add to our portfolio goes through rigorous margin analysis, demand validation, and compliance verification. We don't chase trends — we build sustainable, data-backed inventory strategies that generate consistent sell-through.

"We believe that the best supplier relationships are built on transparency, reliability, and mutual growth. Our goal is to be the partner you never have to worry about."

01
Compliance First
Every product we sell is sourced through authorized channels. We maintain full documentation of invoices, authorization letters, and supplier agreements to protect your brand integrity on Amazon.
02
Data-Driven Sourcing
Our purchasing decisions are backed by market analysis tools and historical sales data. We identify products with proven demand, healthy margins, and low competitive friction before committing capital.
03
Operational Reliability
Consistent reorders, on-time payments, and clear communication. We treat supplier relationships as long-term partnerships, not one-time transactions.
04
Scalable Volume
Our infrastructure is designed to grow with our partners. As sell-through proves out, we scale purchasing volume predictably — giving you a reliable demand channel through Amazon's marketplace.
How We Work

Simple Process.
Serious Execution.

We keep the partnership process straightforward. From first contact to recurring orders, here's how we operate.

📋
STEP 01
Discovery
You share your catalog and pricing. We analyze product fit, margins, and Amazon marketplace demand to identify the best opportunities.
🤝
STEP 02
Agreement
We establish account terms, minimum orders, and pricing structures. Full documentation including resale certificates and business verification provided upfront.
🚀
STEP 03
Execution
Orders placed, product shipped to Amazon FBA centers, and sold to end consumers. We provide recurring purchasing and transparent reporting on sell-through performance.
