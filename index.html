About Us

Built on Discipline.
Driven by Data.

Carlos and Fabiola Veliz started FC Global Group LLC out of Knoxville, Tennessee. The idea was straightforward: buy authorized products in bulk and sell them through Amazon FBA in a way that suppliers can actually rely on.

Before we buy anything, we check the margin, the demand history, and whether we can source it cleanly. We'd rather pass on a category than take a flier on something that looked good for two months.

"We want to be the kind of account you don't have to chase down. Pay on time, order consistently, no surprises."

01
Compliance First
We buy only through authorized channels. Every order comes with invoices, authorization letters, and supplier agreements on file. If your brand ever gets flagged on Amazon, we can show the paper trail on every unit we've sold.
02
Data-Driven Sourcing
Before we commit to a product, we pull sales data and check actual Amazon demand. We want to see consistent velocity and margins that make sense — not a category that spiked once and might again.
03
Operational Reliability
We reorder regularly, pay on time, and actually respond when you reach out. We want accounts we can work with for a long time, so we try to behave like it from day one.
04
Scalable Volume
When a product's selling well, we buy more. The goal is to become a meaningful channel for your brand — not a buyer who disappears after one trial order.
